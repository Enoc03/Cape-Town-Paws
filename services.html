Our Services

Cape Town Paws supports animals through rescue, rehabilitation, adoption and community involvement.

Animal Rescue

We help rescue abandoned, neglected and injured animals that need safety, care and protection.

Rehabilitation

Rescued animals receive care and support while they recover and prepare for a healthier future.

Adoption

We help suitable animals find responsible and loving homes where they can feel safe and cared for.

Community Education

We encourage responsible pet ownership and help members of the community understand how they can support animal welfare.

Want to Help?

You can support Cape Town Paws through adoption, volunteering, fostering, donations or sponsorship.
